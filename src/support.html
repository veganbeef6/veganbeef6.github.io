Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Our games don't use accounts, so uninstalling a game or clearing its data removes your progress. Android's own backup, or an iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad, may restore it if backup is turned on for your device.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Purchases go through Google Play on Android and through the App Store on iPhone and iPad. Close and reopen the game first. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the game's name and your order number: on Google Play it starts with GPA; on the App Store it is on your Apple receipt email. Please don't send card details.

I reinstalled the game on iPhone or iPad. How do I get Remove Ads back?

Open the game's Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Gems and other items that are used up when you spend them can't be restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On Android, follow Google's refund steps. On iPhone and iPad, follow Apple's refund steps.

Can I remove ads?

Many of our games sell Remove Ads or No Ads in the game's shop. Optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

How do I change my ad choices?

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, games that show Google's consent form let you change your choice in the game's Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game to delete what it saved on your phone. We don't keep accounts or a player database. The privacy policy explains what our ad and analytics partners collect. For anything else, email us.
