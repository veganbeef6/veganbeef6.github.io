The short version

  • None of our games asks you to create an account.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device.
  • Our games use advertising and analytics partners, and those partners collect device identifiers and usage data. They are all named below.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

VeganBeef ("we", "us") makes free-to-play mobile games for Android. This policy covers every game on our Google Play developer page and this website. It explains what information is used, why, and what you can do about it. If you choose to play our games, you agree to the collection and use of information described here.

What stays on your device

Your game saves your progress on your device: things like levels, coins, items and settings. We do not receive this data and it is not shared with any company or individual. Deleting the game deletes it. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account, which is controlled by your device settings.

What is collected automatically

Our games use third-party services for advertising and analytics. These services may collect information used to identify your device. Not every game uses every service.

Log data

If an error happens in a game, we collect data through third-party products on your phone, called log data. It may include your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app, the time and date of your use, and other statistics. We do not share this information with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy.

Cookies

Cookies are small files commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. Our games do not use cookies directly, but they may use third-party code and libraries that use cookies to collect information and improve their services. You can accept or refuse these cookies. If you refuse them, some parts of a game may not work.

Ads and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, games that use Google's consent form ask for your choice about personalised ads when they first start. Where it applies, you can change your choice later in the game's Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.
  • Where a game offers Remove Ads, buying it stops banner and interstitial ads. Optional reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation so that we can deliver what you bought.

Why information is used

Information is used to run and improve our games, show and measure ads, understand how the games are played, and find and fix errors. We do not use or share your information with any individual or company other than as described in this policy.

Service providers

We work with third-party companies to help run our games, provide services on our behalf and analyse how the games are used. These companies have access to information so that they can do this work, and they are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose. Each one's handling of data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not run accounts or keep a player database of our own. Saved game data stays on your device until you delete the game. Data collected by our partners is kept and deleted according to their own policies, linked above. To remove what is on your device, uninstall the game. To limit what is linked to your device, reset or delete your advertising ID. For any other request about your data, email us.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. Game trailers load from YouTube only when you press play, and Google's privacy policy then applies to that video.

Security

We value your trust and use commercially acceptable means of protecting information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

Our games and this website may link to other sites. We do not operate those sites and have no control over their content, privacy policies or practices, so please review their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we delete it immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you know that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can act on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted here.
