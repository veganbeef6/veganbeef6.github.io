The short version

  • None of our games asks you to create an account.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device.
  • Our games use advertising and analytics partners, and those partners collect device identifiers and usage data. They are all named below.
  • Payments go through the store you got the game from: Google Play, or Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad. We never see your payment details.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Who we are

VeganBeef ("we", "us") makes free-to-play mobile games for Android, and for iPhone and iPad. This policy covers every game on our Google Play developer page, our games on Apple's App Store (where they are published under the name Ali Shehroz, the owner of ASGAMES), and this website. It explains what information is used, why, and what you can do about it. If you choose to play our games, you agree to the collection and use of information described here.

Some of our games are played by several people on one device, for example 2 3 4 Player Games: 1v1 Party, where one to four players share one phone. These games have no online play, chat or accounts: nothing a player does in the game is shared with other people.

What stays on your device

Your game saves your progress on your device: things like levels, coins, items and settings. We do not receive this data and it is not shared with any company or individual. Deleting the game deletes it. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account, and on iPhone and iPad an iCloud or computer backup may include it; both are controlled by your device settings.

iPhone and iPad

Rocket Kingdoms: Castle Siege is our game for iPhone and iPad. On Apple devices it uses only these services:

Unity AdsAds, served through AdMob
Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote ConfigUnderstanding how the game is played; adjusting ad settings without an update

The data these services collect on iPhone and iPad is:

  • device identifiers: the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • how you use the game, including gameplay events and how you interact with ads
  • diagnostics and crash data
  • an approximate location worked out from your IP address, used for ads
  • purchase history: which items were bought, not how they were paid for

It is used for advertising, analytics and to make the game work. The game does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and it has no accounts. Google Analytics keeps this game's event-level data for two months and user-level data for up to 14 months, then deletes it automatically.

Log data

If an error happens in a game, we collect data through third-party products on your phone, called log data. It may include your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app, the time and date of your use, and other statistics. We do not share this information with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy.

Cookies

Cookies are small files commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. Our games do not use cookies directly, but they may use third-party code and libraries that use cookies to collect information and improve their services. You can accept or refuse these cookies. If you refuse them, some parts of a game may not work.

Ads and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, games that use Google's consent form ask for your choice about personalised ads when they first start. Where it applies, you can change your choice later in the game's Settings (in Rocket Kingdoms: Settings, then Privacy choices).
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before it uses the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you don't allow it, the IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Where a game offers Remove Ads, buying it stops banner and interstitial ads. Optional reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone and iPad. We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of which item was bought, so that we can deliver it.

Why information is used

Information is used to run and improve our games, show and measure ads, understand how the games are played, and find and fix errors. We don't sell your personal information, and we do not use or share it with any individual or company other than as described in this policy.

Service providers

We work with third-party companies to help run our games, provide services on our behalf and analyse how the games are used. These companies have access to information so that they can do this work, and they are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose. Each one's handling of data is covered by its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

We do not run accounts or keep a player database of our own. Saved game data stays on your device until you delete the game. Data collected by our partners is kept and deleted according to their own policies, linked above. To remove what is on your device, uninstall the game. To limit what is linked to your device, reset or delete your advertising ID on Android, or turn off tracking on iPhone and iPad. To ask us to delete analytics data about you, or for any other request about your data, email us with the game's name and the kind of device you play on, and we will act on it with our partners.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. Game trailers load from YouTube only when you press play, and Google's privacy policy then applies to that video.

Security

We value your trust and use commercially acceptable means of protecting information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

Our games and this website may link to other sites. We do not operate those sites and have no control over their content, privacy policies or practices, so please review their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we delete it immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you know that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can act on it.
